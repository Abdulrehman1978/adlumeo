## Brand & Style

This design system translates the imperative "Attention into Growth" into a visceral, cinematic digital experience. Designed for an ultra-creative digital growth, content creation, and advertising agency, the visual language departs entirely from safe corporate SaaS patterns and derivative bento grids. It communicates supreme cultural relevance, technical precision, and unrelenting creative voltage.

The style fuses **High-Contrast Editorial** and **Cinematic Neo-Brutalism**:
- Deep obsidian depths establish an architectural void.
- High-frequency accents—electric hyper-lime and infrared vermilion—act as tactical laser burns that direct eye movement and telegraph decisive action.
- Monumental typographic scale paired with razor-sharp micro-technical accents creates rhythm: sweeping cinematic titles collide with data-driven monospaced coordinates, metrics, and timestamps.
- Textures remain tactile, atmospheric, and dense with editorial intention, utilizing ultra-subtle optical grain, luminous edge borders, and stark monochromatic contrasts.

## Colors

The palette leverages high-dynamic-range contrasts across an inky black canvas:

- **Primary (`#CCFF00` - Hyper-Lime):** The primary activator. Reserved for maximum impact interactions, primary conversions, active filter states, and live performance metrics. It cuts cleanly through dark tones.
- **Secondary (`#FF3E24` - Infrared Vermilion):** The emotional tension accent. Applied to live broadcast badges, high-priority notifications, alert states, and secondary visual counterpoints.
- **Tertiary (`#F5F4EE` - Parchment Ivory):** High-prestige editorial neutral. Serves as primary typography color, delicate dividers, and inverse light panel treatments to ground the high-voltage neon accents with warmth and luxury.
- **Neutral Core (`#0B0C10` - Deep Ink Obsidian):** The foundational base canvas. Paired with elevated container tiers (`#12131A` and `#1A1C24`) and muted silver-metallic accents (`#8E929E` / `#B8BAC2`) for secondary metadata and border architectures.

Use hyper-lime and infrared vermilion with strict restraint; they must never overpower the editorial balance of obsidian and parchment ivory.

## Typography

Typography drives the agency's narrative tension:

- **Syne** commands the headline hierarchy with an experimental, wide-profile posture. It exudes artistic defiance, editorial prestige, and cultural swagger. Headlines run tight line heights with negative tracking to produce physical presence on screen.
- **Hanken Grotesk** serves body text with crystal-clear neutrality. It delivers effortless legibility for long-form case study narratives, strategic pitch briefs, and performance summaries without fighting display elements.
- **JetBrains Mono** introduces razor-sharp technical instrumentation. Used for metadata tags, timestamps, dynamic campaign ROI counters, coordinates, and micro-navigation markers, giving the impression of an elite creative terminal.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout structure pairs disciplined architectural foundations with calculated asymmetric tension:

- **Grid Framework:** A 12-column fluid grid on desktop (`> 1024px`) with `3.5rem` margins and `1.5rem` gutters. Tablet (`768px - 1023px`) transitions to an 8-column layout with `2rem` margins. Mobile (`< 768px`) collapses to a 4-column layout with `1.25rem` margins.
- **Asymmetric Editorial Rhythm:** Editorial blocks break conventional symmetry. Large display titles intentionally overlap grid tracks, paired with tight, technical sidebars. Full-bleed video panels cut between structured data grids.
- **Micro-Macro Spacing:** Layouts alternate between compressed technical groupings (`space-xs` to `space-sm` for data telemetry) and monumental negative space (`space-xl` and above) around cinematic imagery and statements to let high-impact content breathe.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is achieved through structural layering and luminous edge geometry rather than soft skeuomorphic shadows:

- **Tonal Layers:**
  - Base Floor: `#0B0C10` (Canvas deep ink)
  - Surface Tier 1: `#12131A` (Case cards, content frames)
  - Surface Tier 2: `#1A1C24` (Hover states, modals, floating docks)
- **Luminous Low-Contrast Outlines:** Surfaces are bounded by razor-thin 1px borders tinted with `#F5F4EE` at 8% opacity (`rgba(245, 244, 238, 0.08)`). On hover, critical borders transition to sharp `#CCFF00` or `#FF3E24` accents.
- **Atmospheric Glow & Edge Halos:** Interactive states project an intense, highly diffused back-glow (`0px 0px 40px rgba(204, 255, 0, 0.15)`) mimicking stage lighting and backlit screens.
- **Glass Optics:** Overlay sheets, sticky headers, and floating audio/reel docks utilize `backdrop-filter: blur(20px)` over `#0B0C10` at 75% opacity with an ultra-fine internal rim light.

## Shapes

The design system enforces a uncompromising **0px Sharp** geometric discipline (`roundedness: 0`):

- All interactive containers, buttons, cards, visual frames, and modal sheets are built with razor-sharp 90-degree corners.
- This creates an architectural, unapologetic silhouette that reinforces brutalist precision and distinct editorial sophistication.
- Selected badges and technical chip indicators utilize an engineered chamfer (45-degree angled clipped corners via CSS clip-path) to evoke technical hardware instruments without reverting to soft, generic pill shapes.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action:** Solid `#CCFF00` block fill with `#0B0C10` text set in `JetBrains Mono` bold uppercase. Instantaneous inverse transition on hover (`#F5F4EE` fill with black text) with zero corner radius.
- **Secondary Ghost:** 1px border of `#F5F4EE` at 20% opacity with `#F5F4EE` text. On hover, border snaps to `#CCFF00` with text shifting to `#CCFF00` and an inner technical arrow icon sliding 4px forward.
- **Destructive/Critical:** `#FF3E24` stroke or solid fill for high-tension and live broadcast operations.

### Cards & Case Study Showcases
- Sharp, monolith rectangles built on `#12131A` with 1px borders of `rgba(245, 244, 238, 0.08)`.
- Features full-bleed cinematic video headers, monospaced metadata counters (e.g., `[CAMPAIGN_04 // +318% ROAS]`), and asymmetric title overlays in `Syne`.
- Hover trigger activates an ultra-slow scale on imagery alongside an ambient hyper-lime edge bloom.

### Form Inputs & Selectors
- Flat obsidian fields with bottom-border-only or structural 1px frame in `rgba(245, 244, 238, 0.15)`.
- Input text rendered in `Hanken Grotesk` 16px; technical indicators, currency toggles, and character counters rendered in `JetBrains Mono` 12px.
- Focus state instantly ignites a solid 1px `#CCFF00` border with a subtle neon bleed.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- Sharp geometric square modules (`16px x 16px`) with 1px border. Checked state fills with `#CCFF00` featuring an obsidian geometric cross or solid inner block fill. No rounded corners.

### Chips & Badges
- High-density tags combining a sharp border, `JetBrains Mono` uppercase labels, and a live pulse indicator (a 4px glowing dot of `#CCFF00` or `#FF3E24`).

### Domain-Specific Components
- **Live Reel Player Dock:** A floating bar containing live waveform audio visualizers, frame counters, and playback scrubbers wrapped in a frosted obsidian glass rail.
- **Performance Ticker:** Continuous marquee banner delivering real-time metrics, client wins, and live advertising spend velocities in oversized italicized display typography.
